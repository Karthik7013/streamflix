## Ways to sign up or sign in

StreamFlix supports three sign-in methods:

- Email and password. New accounts must verify their email address first — you'll receive a verification email, and after clicking the link you're signed in automatically.
- Google account
- GitHub account

All methods create the same StreamFlix account. If you change your password and forget it, you can request a password reset — a reset link is emailed to you and expires after one hour.

## What needs an account

You can browse, search, and watch movies without an account. Signing in unlocks:

- Saving titles to your watchlist
- Requesting movies that aren't in the catalog
- Reporting an issue with a video
- Writing comments and using the settings page

## Account roles

Accounts are either regular users or administrators. Administrators manage the catalog, requests, and reports. Only users with the admin role see the admin area.
