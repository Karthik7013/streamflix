## What it is

The StreamFlix assistant lives on the /ai page. You can ask it questions about movies and anything related to StreamFlix, and it answers in a conversational chat interface.

## What you can ask it

- **Find movies by title or keyword** — "Show me The Godfather" or "action movies"
- **Browse by genre** — "What genres are available?" or "Recommend a good sci-fi movie"
- **Search by plot, theme, or vibe** — describe a movie you half-remember ("a movie about someone stranded on Mars") and it finds matches from the catalog
- **Platform questions** — how to use watchlists, requests, reporting, and other StreamFlix features

## How results appear

When the assistant looks something up, results render automatically as movie cards in the chat — you don't need to click links. If it can't find anything, it will say so and suggest trying a different search.

## Tips

Use the suggested question chips to get started and the retry button to re-ask if a response fails. The assistant is free to use.
