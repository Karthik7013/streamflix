## What is StreamFlix?

StreamFlix is a streaming platform where you can watch movies and short-form clips online. It is built to work like a modern streaming service: browse a curated catalog, open a title, and play it in a built-in video player. You don't need to install anything — everything runs in your browser.

## What can you do on StreamFlix?

- Browse the catalog through the home page, explore pages, and genre tags
- Search for movies by title or keyword
- Get recommendations by genre or by describing the plot or vibe you're after
- Watch movies with a full video player, including trailers
- Keep a personal watchlist of titles you want to come back to
- Request movies that aren't in the catalog yet
- Ask the built-in AI assistant questions about movies and the platform

## Is StreamFlix free?

Yes. Browsing, searching, and watching are all free. You only need a free account for a few things, like saving a watchlist, requesting movies, commenting, and reporting issues.
