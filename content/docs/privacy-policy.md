The full, legally binding text is published at /privacy. This summary covers the key points.

- When you create an account we collect your name and email address. Optionally you may add a profile avatar. As you use the Service we record your favorite titles, watch history, and comments. We also collect standard technical data such as browser type, device information, and IP address through server logs and analytics.
- We use this information to maintain your account, personalize your experience, respond to support requests, improve the Service through anonymous usage analysis, and send essential service communications such as password reset emails.
- We do not sell, trade, or share personal information with third parties for marketing. Trusted providers include Better Auth (authentication), Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights (anonymous usage analytics), and the Internet Archive (media source; no user data is shared with it).
- Data is stored in encrypted databases with encryption in transit and at rest. Passwords are hashed and salted and never stored in plain text.
- We use only essential cookies for authentication and session management. No tracking, advertising, or third-party cross-site cookies.
- Your rights include accessing your data, correcting inaccuracies, deleting your account and associated data through Settings, exporting your data in a portable format, and withdrawing consent.
- Data is retained while your account is active. After account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days. Server logs with IP addresses are retained no more than 30 days; anonymized aggregates may be kept indefinitely.
- For questions or to exercise your rights, contact us via the contact page. We respond within two business days.
