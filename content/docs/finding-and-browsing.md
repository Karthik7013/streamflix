## The home page

The home page opens with a featured hero carousel and a Top 10 row of popular titles. If you're signed in, it also shows a row of movies from your watchlist. Everything on the home page is browsable without an account.

## Explore and genres

The Explore page gives you a search bar plus a grid of genre tags (for example, action, comedy, sci-fi). Clicking a tag opens that genre's page with its movies. Search and browse are public — you don't need to sign in.

## Searching

You can search for movies by title or keyword. The search bar is available on the Explore page, and there's a quick search command palette too. Search matches movie titles and associated tags, so searching "thriller" returns tagged thrillers.

## Related content

Each movie page shows you related titles so you can keep discovering new things to watch after you finish a movie you liked.
