StreamFlix has dedicated channels for different inquiry types. Choose the right one for the fastest reply.

- General inquiries, feedback, or partnership requests: hello@streamflix.app.
- DMCA and copyright takedown notices: dmca@streamflix.app. Use this dedicated email, not the general channel. See the DMCA policy for what a valid notice must include.
- Technical support (trouble using the Service): support@streamflix.app.
- Legal correspondence and official documents: use the "StreamFlix" legal entity channel on the contact page; contact information for legal matters is available upon request.
- We aim to respond to all inquiries within two business days. For urgent matters, include "Urgent" in your email subject line.
