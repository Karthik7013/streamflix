## Saving movies

Any movie in the catalog can be added to your watchlist with a single tap on its bookmark icon. Movies appear in your watchlist whether you browse the home page, a genre page, or a movie's own page.

## Where to find your watchlist

Signed-in users have three ways to reach their saved titles:

- The My Watchlist row on the home page
- The Watchlist page
- The My Watchlist link in Settings

Opening a saved movie takes you straight to its page so you can continue watching.

## Requirements

A free account is required to save or view a watchlist. Visitors who aren't signed in are prompted to log in when they try to use it. Removing a movie from your watchlist is instant.
