## Playing a movie

Open any movie in the catalog and look for the play/trailer controls on its page, or go to the title's watch page. Movies stream directly in your browser — no account or download needed.

## The player

StreamFlix uses a custom player with ambient lighting effects around the video. It provides the usual controls: play/pause, a progress bar, volume, and fullscreen.

## Keyboard shortcuts

While watching, you can use these shortcuts:

- Space or K — play / pause
- ← / → — seek backward / forward 10 seconds
- ↑ / ↓ — volume up / down
- M — mute / unmute
- F — toggle fullscreen
- ? — toggle the shortcuts help panel
- Esc — close

Press ? any time to see this list in the player.

## Troubleshooting playback

If a title fails to load, the player shows a "This title is temporarily unavailable" message with a Try again button. If problems persist, use the Report an issue option on the movie's page.
