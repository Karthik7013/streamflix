## What are Shorts?

Shorts are short-form video clips on StreamFlix, similar to vertical short-video feeds. They're a quick way to sample content without committing to a full movie. Shorts run in a full-screen feed on the Shorts page — available from the main navigation.

## How the feed works

Shorts autoplay one at a time. The clip that's currently in view plays on loop (muted), and as you move through the feed the next clip takes over. Each clip may show a title and a poster while it loads.

## Requirements

Shorts are free to watch and don't require an account, just like movies.
