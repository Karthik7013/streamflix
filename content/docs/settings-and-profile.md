## Where Settings are

The Settings page is available from the main navigation after you sign in. It's where you manage your profile and account.

## Profile

You can update your display name and upload a profile avatar and a cover photo. Uploads update instantly and show up across the site.

## Password and watchlist

- **Change password** — update the password for an email-based account.
- **My Watchlist** — a shortcut to every movie you've saved.

## Signing out and closing your account

The Account section lets you sign out of your current session. It also has a permanent **Delete Account** option: deleting is immediate and can't be undone, so the flow asks you to type `delete-my-account` to confirm. After deletion you're signed out automatically.

## Admin accounts

If your account is an administrator, Settings also shows a navigation entry to the admin area where the catalog, requests, and reports are managed.
