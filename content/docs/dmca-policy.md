The full, legally binding text is published at /dmca. This summary covers the key points.

- StreamFlix complies with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act. If you believe content on the Service infringes your copyright, submit a written DMCA notice to the designated Copyright Agent at dmca@streamflix.app. We acknowledge receipt within two business days.
- A valid notice must include your physical or electronic signature; identification of the copyrighted work; identification of the infringing material with a specific URL locating it on the Service; your contact information (full name, mailing address, phone, and email); a good-faith belief that the use is not authorized; and a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
- If your material was removed by mistake or misidentification, you may file a counter-notification with the Copyright Agent. It must include your signature; identification of the removed material and where it appeared; a good-faith belief statement under penalty of perjury; and your name, address, and phone number plus consent to the jurisdiction of the federal district court for your district.
- Users subject to more than one valid DMCA notice are treated as repeat infringers and their accounts may be terminated.
- Media content on StreamFlix comes from publicly accessible third-party archives, including the Internet Archive. StreamFlix does not host, upload, or control the content displayed.
- For questions about the DMCA policy, contact us via the contact page.
