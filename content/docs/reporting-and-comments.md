## Reporting an issue

Every movie page has a "Report an issue" section. Use it when something is wrong with a title — for example, the video won't play, the audio is out of sync, or the wrong video is attached. Describe the problem in up to 1,000 characters.

You must be signed in to report an issue. Once submitted, a confirmation is shown and the report goes to an administrator for review.

## Comments on movies

Movie pages have a comments section where people discuss the title:

- Reading comments is public — anyone can view them, signed in or not.
- Writing a comment requires a free account. If you're signed out, you'll be asked to log in.
- Comments show the author's name, avatar, and how long ago they were posted.

Be respectful — abusive or spammy comments may be removed by administrators.
